# The second well (HoMM2, base game)

Two saves of the base-game map of `BuiltThisTurn`, made from its `built_this_turn_00_baseline` (day 1): every castle of a player was given the Well and the faction's second well (the Farm for a Knight), then the week was ended.
They show how many creatures the two buildings add each week (`docs/HOMM2_SAVE_FORMAT.md`, "The Well and the second well"): the Well +2 in every built dwelling, the second well +8 in the first dwelling only.

The game only lists save names of at most 8 characters before the extension; the second column is the name the game gave the save.

| Save | Name in the game | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| second_well_01_well__farm | `WELLFARM.GM1` | Day 1: the Well and the second well built in the castles; nothing has grown yet (the dwellings of the other castles were bought out). Xabran (Knight) holds 12 peasants and 8 archers |
| second_well_02_next_week | `WFW2.GM1` | Week 2, day 1: Xabran holds 34 peasants and 18 archers; the other castles show the same rule for their factions |
